常见问题

需要注册账号吗？
不需要。所有记录保存在本机，不经过我们的服务器。
换手机时怎么带走记录？
iPhone 上可以在设置里打开 iCloud 同步；也可以在设置 →「备份」导出备份文件，到新设备上用「恢复」导入。备份里包含原图。
粘贴的链接识别不出来？
设置 →「支持平台」列出内置识别规则的平台。其它网页会尽量读出能用的信息，读不全的字段可以手动补上。部分平台需要登录才能看到的页面无法识别。
分享海报会暴露座位和票价吗？
不会。座位、票价、同行人默认不印，只有你在分享工坊里主动打开时才会出现。
能从表格批量导入吗？
可以。设置 →「表格导入」读取 UTF-8 编码的 CSV；Excel 文件请先另存为「CSV UTF-8」。
在电脑上做模版
浏览器版模版工坊可以设计海报模版，导出的 .lmtemplate 文件用隔空投送发到 iPhone 即可在 Livemark 里打开。

Support

Livemark is a journal for the shows you go to — concerts, club gigs, festivals, plays, musicals, films and exhibitions. Paste a ticket link to fill in the details, turn a show into a ticket-stub poster, and look back with a year in review.

For problems or suggestions, please open an issue on GitHub Issues with your device model, OS version and app version (shown in Settings). If a link isn't recognized, include the link.

FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. Everything is stored on your device and never passes through our servers.
How do I move my entries to a new phone?
On iPhone, turn on iCloud Sync in Settings. Or export a backup file from Settings → Backup and import it on the new device with Restore. Backups include your original photos.
A pasted link isn't recognized
Settings → Supported platforms lists the sites with built-in rules. For other pages Livemark reads what it can, and you can fill in the rest by hand. Pages that require signing in can't be read.
Will a shared poster show my seat or price?
No. Seat, price and companions are left off unless you turn them on in the share studio.
Can I import from a spreadsheet?
Yes. Settings → Import table reads UTF-8 CSV files; save Excel files as “CSV UTF-8” first.
Designing templates on a computer
The browser template editor designs poster templates; AirDrop the exported .lmtemplate file to your iPhone to open it in Livemark.
