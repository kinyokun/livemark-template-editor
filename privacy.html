隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 25 日

Livemark 是一款记录观演经历的 App。我们不收集你的任何个人数据：没有账号，没有我们自己的服务器，不含广告，也不含任何第三方统计或追踪 SDK。

数据存在哪里

你的记录、照片、海报模版与设置都保存在你的设备上。只有在你开启以下功能时，数据才会离开设备：

  • iCloud 同步（可选，仅 iOS）：记录保存在你自己的 iCloud 云盘中的 Livemark 文件夹，由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。我们无法访问。
  • 导出备份与表格：文件由你自己选择保存或分享到哪里。

会联网的几种情况

  • 识别链接：当你粘贴或分享一个网页链接，Livemark 会直接请求该网页，在设备上读出演出名称、时间、场馆与封面。请求直接发往该网站，不经过我们。
  • 地点与地图：iOS 上的地图与地址查询使用 Apple 地图；Android 上的地图底图来自 OpenFreeMap。为中国大陆以外的场馆查找位置时，Livemark 会把你输入的场馆名、城市与国家/地区发送给 OpenStreetMap 的 Nominatim 服务。这项查询可在设置里的「海外定位」关闭。不会发送你的坐标或记录里的其它内容。
  • 歌单导入：你主动导入公开歌单时，Livemark 会请求你提供的歌单页面。

权限

  • 相册：读取你选择的照片作为封面、现场照片或海报图层；保存你导出的海报。
  • 通知：在你开启开演提醒时使用，提醒内容只含名称、时间与地点。
  • 日历：「加入日历」打开系统的新建事件页；Livemark 不读取你日历里的内容。

Livemark 不使用相机、麦克风，也不获取你的位置。

私人信息

座位、票价、同行人等字段只保存在你的设备（或你的 iCloud）上。分享海报、小组件与提醒默认都不包含它们，只有你在对应界面主动打开时才会印出。

儿童

Livemark 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会更新本页并修改生效日期。有任何问题，请在 GitHub Issues 留言。

Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Livemark is an app for keeping a record of the shows you attend. We do not collect any personal data: there is no account, no server of our own, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDK.

Where your data lives

Your entries, photos, poster templates and settings are stored on your device. Data only leaves the device when you use one of these features:

  • iCloud sync (optional, iOS only): entries are kept in a Livemark folder in your own iCloud Drive and handled by Apple under its privacy policy. We cannot access them.
  • Backup and table export: you choose where the file is saved or shared.

When the app goes online

  • Reading links: when you paste or share a web link, Livemark requests that page directly and extracts the title, date, venue and cover on your device. The request goes straight to that website, not through us.
  • Places and maps: on iOS, maps and address lookups use Apple Maps; on Android, map tiles come from OpenFreeMap. When looking up a venue outside mainland China, Livemark sends the venue name, city and country/region you entered to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service. You can turn this off with “Overseas locations” in Settings. Your location and the rest of your entries are never sent.
  • Setlist import: when you import a public playlist, Livemark requests the page you provided.

Permissions

  • Photos: to read the photos you pick as covers, show photos or poster layers, and to save posters you export.
  • Notifications: for show reminders you turn on; reminders contain only the title, time and place.
  • Calendar: “Add to Calendar” opens the system's new-event sheet; Livemark does not read your calendar.

Livemark does not use the camera or microphone and does not access your location.

Private details

Seat, price and companion fields stay on your device (or in your iCloud). Shared posters, widgets and reminders leave them out unless you explicitly turn them on.

Children

Livemark is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the effective date. For questions, please open an issue on GitHub Issues.
